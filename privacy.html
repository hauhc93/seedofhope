Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Seed of Hope is a self-care app. It is not a medical device, a therapy service or a crisis service.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Seed of Hope has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Everything you enter is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app:

  • Your name, chosen avatar and an optional helper contact
  • Daily mood and energy check-ins
  • Journal entries
  • Small wins and the growth of your Seed
  • Up to 3 trusted contacts (name and phone number)
  • Reminder settings

How your data is used

Your data is used only to show you your own garden, history and journal inside the app.

  • Calls and messages: when you tap Call or Send SOS Message, the app opens your phone's own Phone or Messages app. Seed of Hope does not send or read any message itself.
  • Reminders: reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS.
  • Safety check: when you save a journal entry, the app checks the text on your device for words that may signal a crisis, so it can gently show support options. The result is not stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data. We cannot see your data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Seed of Hope is not directed to children under 13.

If you are in danger

Seed of Hope is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number now.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if cloud sync or AI features are added), we will update this page and ask for your consent in the app before any data leaves your device.
