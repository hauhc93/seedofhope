Seed of Hope is a quiet iPhone app for difficult days. Take one small act of care, and watch your Seed of Hope grow. No streaks, no penalties, and your seed never dies.

If you are in danger: Seed of Hope is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number now, or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
