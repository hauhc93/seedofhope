Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Email seedofhope.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

If you are in danger: Seed of Hope is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number now, or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Seed of Hope has no account and no server, so we cannot see or recover your data.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. This removes everything it stored.

I missed some days. Did I lose progress?

No. Your seed never shrinks and never dies. Come back whenever you are ready.

How do I change my trusted contacts?

Open the support (SOS) screen from the Garden tab. You can add up to 3 people and remove them at any time.
